# THE DAILY WIRE — Format

## What an issue is
A single numbered broadsheet card: a bold headline, the story in a few lines, and the day's numbers — the operation reporting on itself, in the open. One issue per beat; issues run in waves across Instagram, Threads, the Vice City Wire Facebook group, and the Discord #announcements channel.

## The card
- Newspaper broadsheet styling: masthead "THE DAILY WIRE," issue number, dateline.
- Headline first — a bold statement, never scene-setting.
- The story under it in plain lines: what happened, the numbers, who it touches.
- JPG for posting (some uploaders reject WEBP).

## From newspaper to ticket
The newspaper is the ticket program's raw material:
1. An issue's article becomes the FACE of a LIVE WIRE TICKET™ — the face states what the piece is.
2. The ticket pushes in (~2s, drumroll/hype), then a white-flash "amperage" cut wires straight into the reel.
3. The ticket back closes: "THE WIRE CARRIED IT LIVE" · follow @vicecitywire92 · PASS THIS TICKET ON.
4. From Nov 22, 2026, the newsletter reels open with the ticket going from CRINKLED to OPEN into the reel.

Ticket template and license: https://github.com/whitedg35-beep/live-wire-ticket

## The market note
Posted plain, the cards are the operation's diary. As a format, they're a product: any clipper, streamer, or community can run their own Daily Wire under license — their news, our press.
